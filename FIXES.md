# SaveBucks fixes

- Settings navigation now works from its separate sidebar section.
- Currency and income controls are available in Settings; choose the currency display and add/edit monthly income.
- Savings goals use a clear target/current-saved/monthly contribution form, a live timeline estimate, and a working contribution logger.
- Collapsed desktop navigation is icon-only, aligned, and has hover labels; mobile drawer behavior is unchanged.

Currency selection changes formatting only and does not convert previously entered amounts.
